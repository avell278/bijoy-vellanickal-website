About Bijoy

Real estate that feels like family

Bijoy Vellanickal is a Sales Representative with RE/MAX Centre City Realty Inc., serving London and communities across southwestern Ontario. His deep understanding of these vibrant neighbourhoods, combined with genuine passion for real estate, makes him the ideal partner in your home-buying or selling journey.

What sets Bijoy apart is how he communicates — like a family member, not a salesperson. His calm demeanor and deep market knowledge turn what could be a stressful process into something simple and smooth. He answers questions at every step, gives sound advice, and stays there to guide you long after the deal closes.

Many first-time homebuyers and sellers share the same story: dedication, patience, and a process that felt easy from start to finish.
